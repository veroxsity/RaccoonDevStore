ADFYT runs YouTube's TV interface inside a native Xbox app. It removes video and home screen ads before YouTube's player schedules them, gives the controller proper navigation and back behaviour, and requests 1080p instead of the 720p TV default.

## Controls

- D-pad or either stick to navigate
- A to select
- B to go back and close a video
- LB and RB for previous and next video
- Menu to return home

## Before installing

Built and tested on an Xbox Series S in Developer Mode. Series X and Xbox One have not been tested. Sign in with YouTube's normal TV pairing code, or use it signed out.
